**Candidate D-dual — `ternary_1step_ds` (`src/quant_rnd/schemes.py`):**
`_ternary_lloyd_fit` with dual=True, n_iter=1 — heuristic dual thresholds
(conditional means per side) -> one per-side L2-optimal refit ->
reassignment. Same 1.835 bpw at group 128 as ternary_lloyd_ds

n_iter exposed for the convergence check.

**Numbers (seeds 7–9, clean + skew 0.5, 64 groups, capture of
ternary_lloyd_ds's win over dual_scale_ternary):**

| seed | skew | n=1 | n=2 | n=3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 0.0 | 0.83 | 0.96 | 0.98 |
| 7 | 0.5 | 0.73 | 0.84 | 0.87 |
| 8 | 0.0 | 0.85 | 0.97 | 0.99 |
| 8 | 0.5 | 0.78 | 0.89 | 0.90 |
| 9 | 0.0 | 0.55 | 0.63 | 0.65 |
| 9 | 0.5 | 0.57 | 0.67 | 0.76 |

**Verdict: PARTIAL.** One iteration does NOT replicate the symmetric
twin's 0.84–0.87 capture; the dual case converges slower. 2–3 fixed
iterations (still O(1), no convergence loop) get 0.63–0.99 — so the
practical dual-scale encoder is 2–3 iterations, not 1. Seed 9 skew 0.5
is the slowest (needs n=5 for full convergence) — a slow-convergence
case worth understanding when adaptive stopping is designed.

**Scratch-methodology note:** my first scratch measurement ran the dual
scheme under an unregistered QuantResult name and reconstruct() fell
back to single-scale decoding, underestimating the dual 1-step by
~0.1 capture. The landed numbers use the registered name. Lesson: the
_DUAL_SCALE_SCHEMES registration is load-bearing for correctness —
worth a comment, which is now in the scheme docstring.

## Engineering session 2026-09-21 ~01:45 PDT — prefill roofline

**Picked:** unchecked backlog item "extend opcount.py with a prefill
(compute-bound) roofline" — the regime where the roadmap predicted
ternary's op advantage becomes first-order. Direct continuation of the
opcount thread.

**Landed:** commit `7ff4fd9` (+ GitHub mirror 2bfd47c74f80). Suite 91/91
green (+1 test).

**`prefill_roofline_tps()`** (`src/quant_rnd/opcount.py`): prompt-level
roofline — weights streamed once, activations at an estimated
6*d_model elems/token/layer, KV write for the prompt, FLOPs counted as
adds+muls per weight (lookups aren't FLOPs). peak_flops is an explicit
argument (no baked-in constants); tests use Apple-published 5.2 TFLOPS
FP32 M1 Pro GPU. Honest caveats in the docstring: adds count as 1 FLOP
against an FMA-counted peak (conservative for ternary — up to ~2x
headroom on FMA hardware, stated not folded in); attention O(L^2)
ignored (<5% at 4k, known under-count at long ctx).

**Result:** the regime flip is real. L=1: both references bandwidth-bound
(AI 2.8 vs balance 26 FLOP/byte). L=512+: both compute-bound (AI 100x+
balance) and the ceiling ratio is EXACTLY the FLOP-per-weight ratio:
**1.795x ternary_1step over int2_kmeans_q8** (peak, efficiency, and
prompt length all cancel in the test). Note: this is the FLOP-counted
ceiling ratio; the 3.5x energy-proxy ratio is a different (softer)
number — reported separately, not conflated.

**Backlog grown:** Mac-side validation of the prefill ceiling ratio
(needs Justin's Mac); attention O(L^2) term for long context (32k+).
