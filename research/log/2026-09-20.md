# Research log — 2026-09-20 (initial sweep)

Mission kickoff: find a path to run a 70B-class model on Justin's 16GB
M1 Pro Mac. Budget reality check: macOS takes ~4–6GB, leaving ~10–11GB
for weights + KV cache + runtime.

## Findings

1. **llama.cpp now has ternary quants.** TQ1_0/TQ2_0 (1.7–2.1 bits/param)
   and IQ1_S/IQ1_M (1.5–1.75 bpw) exist in the quant zoo. A 70B at TQ1_0
   is ~14.9GB on disk — the smallest a dense 70B GGUF has ever been.
   Open: whether Ollama's bundled llama.cpp supports TQ yet, and whether
   70B TQ1_0 GGUFs exist on HuggingFace.
   Source: https://blog.csdn.net/gitblog_00939/article/details/150958360

2. **Native ternary 70B would be ~13.8GB** (16/1.58 ≈ 10.1x smaller than
   FP16's 140GB) — fits a MacBook Pro in principle, and ternary ops are
   add/sub instead of multiplies, so CPU inference is fast.
   Source: https://github.com/broomva/broomva.tech/blob/HEAD/apps/broomva/content/writing/bitnet-1bit-llms-agentic-edge.mdx

3. **But no good 70B ternary model exists.** Public ternary models top out
   ~8–10B; the Llama3-8B-1.58 conversion (100B tokens) is reported poor
   quality; no 1T-token 70B ternary model is announced as of Sep 2026.
   This is the #1 thing to watch — a proper release changes everything.
   Source: https://github.com/toastmanau/wyltek-industries/blob/HEAD/research/bitnet-well-trained-models-2026.md

4. **Mixed-precision 2-bit MoE is the practical path today.** oQ (oMLX)
   does data-driven per-layer bit allocation: Qwen3.5-35B-A3B at 2-bit
   scores 64% MMLU vs 14% for naive mlx-lm 2-bit; JANG does similar
   adaptive allocation (attention/rounters at 6–8bit, experts at 2–4bit).
   A 35B MoE at effective 2-bit ≈ 8.8GB — fits with room to spare, and
   MoE's few active params decode fast on bandwidth-bound Apple Silicon.
   Sources: https://github.com/jundot/omlx/blob/HEAD/docs/oQ_Quantization.md
   and https://github.com/imfantuan/jangq-mlx

5. **KV-cache compression frees headroom.** VeloxQuant-MLX / TurboQuant
   claim 5–8x KV cache reduction at near-fp16 quality; Ollama has
   `OLLAMA_KV_CACHE_TYPE=q8_0|q4_0` env. Small for 70B (~1.3GB at 4k ctx)
   but every GB counts against the ~10–11GB budget.
   Sources: https://github.com/rajveer43/veloxquant-mlx/blob/HEAD/docs-site/blog/2026-05-28-hands-on.md
   and https://medium.com/@kapildevkhatik2/optimizing-ollama-performance-on-windows-hardware-quantization-parallelism-more-fac04802288e

6. **70B at Q4 is ~40GB** — the "everyone knows" number, confirming dense
   70B at standard quants is ~4x over budget.
   Source: https://github.com/prime-basin-ai/ai-software/blob/HEAD/research/llm-runners/llama.cpp.md
